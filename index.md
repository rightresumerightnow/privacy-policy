# Privacy Policy for Right Resume - Right Now

**Last Updated: May 2026**

## Overview
Right Resume - Right Now is an extension that generates tailored resumes and cover letters for job applications.

## Data We Collect
- **Job Posting Content**: Job titles, company names, requirements, and posting content (processed in real-time)
- **Your Resume Data**: Only what you input into the extension
- **Authentication**: Email address for user account (managed by Bolt Database Auth)

## What We Don't Collect
- We do NOT track your browsing activity
- We do NOT store browsing history
- We do NOT use analytics or tracking pixels
- We do NOT sell or share your data

## How We Use Data
1. **Job Analysis**: Job posting content is sent to Anthropic Claude AI to extract job requirements
2. **Document Generation**: Your resume is processed by Claude AI to generate tailored versions
3. **Storage**: Generated documents are temporarily cached but not permanently stored
4. **Payments**: Payment information is processed securely through Stripe

## Third-Party Services
- **Anthropic Claude API**: Analyzes job postings and generates resumes/cover letters
- **Stripe**: Processes payments for premium features
- **Bolt Database**: Stores user accounts and subscription data with encryption

## Your Privacy Rights
- You can delete your account and all associated data at any time
- You can request your data from our settings page
- You control what information is shared

## Security
- All data is transmitted via HTTPS encryption
- Bolt Database uses industry-standard encryption at rest
- API keys are stored securely and never exposed to the client

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy occasionally. We'll notify users of significant changes.

## Contact
For privacy concerns, contact us through the extension's options page.
